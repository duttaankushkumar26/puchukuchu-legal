Delete your PuchuKuchu account

PuchuKuchu · Last updated September 21, 2026

You can ask for your account, and everything linked to it, to be deleted at any time.

How to delete your account

Email kuchupuchu.support@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm it is you, then delete your account and finish within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, name, emoji and profile photo.
  • Your messages, daily notes, moods, streaks and countdowns.
  • Photos, doodles and notes you added to your Constellation.
  • Your location data and your push notification token.

Your subscription is managed by Google Play, so cancel it there too if you have one.

Only some of your data

If you only want part of your data removed, such as your location history, say so in the same email.
