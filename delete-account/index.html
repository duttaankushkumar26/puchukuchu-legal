Delete your PuchuKuchu account

PuchuKuchu · Last updated September 22, 2026

You can delete your account, and everything you added, at any time.

How to delete your account

In the app: open Settings, choose Delete account, and type DELETE to confirm. The deletion is scheduled for 7 days later. If you change your mind, sign back in before then and choose Keep my account.

If you cannot open the app, email puchukuchu.support@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm it is you and finish within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, name, emoji and profile photo.
  • Your messages, daily notes, moods, streaks and countdowns.
  • Photos, doodles and notes you added to your Constellation.
  • Your location data and your push notification token.
  • Your login.

The files stored for what you added are removed too.

What stays for your partner

What your partner added stays with them, and their account carries on. They get one gentle note that you have left. If nobody is left in the couple, the whole shared space is erased.

Your subscription is managed by Google Play, so cancel it there too if you have one.

Only some of your data

If you only want part of your data removed, such as your location history, say so in an email to puchukuchu.support@gmail.com.
